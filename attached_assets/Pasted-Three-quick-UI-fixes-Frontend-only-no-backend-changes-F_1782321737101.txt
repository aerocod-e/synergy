Fix 1 -- Invisible password fields on profile screen
Open artifacts/mobile/app/(profile)/index.tsx. The three password TextInput fields (Current Password, New Password, Confirm New Password) are currently invisible against the #f5f7fa background because they have no border or background color.
Wrap all three fields and the Save Password button in a white card container:

backgroundColor: '#ffffff'
borderRadius: 12
padding: 16
marginBottom: 16
Standard card shadow consistent with the rest of the app (use theme.shadows.card if it exists)

Each individual TextInput inside the card should have:

backgroundColor: theme.colors.background (#f5f7fa) or a very light gray to distinguish it from the white card
borderRadius: 8
padding: 14
marginBottom: 12
No border needed since the background contrast is sufficient
